Getting ready for your stay

We're Algarve Beach Apartments, the local team managing your stay on the ground here in Albufeira. With decades of experience welcoming holidaymakers to the Algarve, we're with you every step of the way, from now through to the day you check out.

Everything you need before you arrive now lives here in this Manager, rather than in a separate form or email - it's worth a few minutes to look through it:

  • Contact Details - keep your email and phone number up to date, so we can reach you if anything comes up.
  • Arrival & Departure - let us know your flight (or other travel) details as soon as you have them, so we can plan your key handover.
  • Optional Extras - add an Airport Transfer, Cot/High Chair, or Welcome Pack if you'd like one waiting for you.
  • Guest Registrations - Portuguese law requires us to register every guest's ID details ahead of check-in.
  • Location & Check-in and Last Days & Check-out - your address, access details, and everything you need for a smooth arrival and departure.
  • FAQ and Local Guide - answers to common questions and our own recommendations for the local area.

We'll be in touch closer to your arrival with anything else you need, but feel free to look around and update your details any time before then. Thank you for choosing us, and we look forward to welcoming you to Albufeira!
