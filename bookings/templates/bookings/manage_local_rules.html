Local Rules

Albufeira Municipal Council's rules for public places - you're welcome here, but only if you respect the people, the place, and the rules below. These are enforced by the council, not by us, and the fines are real.

Forbidden in public places

  • Full or partial nudity in public - €300 to €1,800
  • Drinking alcohol in the street - €300 to €1,500
  • Urinating in public spaces - €300 to €1,500
  • Loud noise at night - €300 to €1,500
  • Sleeping or camping in public areas - €150 to €750
  • Cooking in public spaces - €150 to €750
  • Spitting on the ground - €150 to €750

Respect the locals. Protect the city. Relax - love Albufeira the right way.
