Failure on the side of the Holidaymaker to make the Balance Payment within the required time frame can be treated as a cancellation of the Booking by the Holidaymaker.

If the Holidaymaker needs to cancel or amend the Booking, the Holidaymaker must telephone Algarve Beach Apartments on the number given in the written confirmation email as soon as possible.

The Holidaymaker will then be required to confirm the cancellation of the Booking by replying to the original written confirmation email sent by Algarve Beach Apartments. As outlined in 1, cancellations made within the Grace Period of seven days after the sending of the original written confirmation of the Booking by Algarve Beach Apartments affords the Holidaymaker the right to a full refund of the Initial Payment.

As equally outlined in 1, beyond the Grace Period, cancellations entitle Algarve Beach Apartments to withhold any previously made Initial Payment and/or Balance Payment.
